# Ice and Fire Dragons — Raise and Ride Your Own Giant Dragon

Ice and Fire Dragons 9minecraft Mod brings giant dragons and mythical creatures to your world. You can find dragon eggs, hatch them, and raise tiny hatchlings into huge beasts. Once they grow up, you can put a saddle on them and fly through the sky. The mod also fills the land and sea with other monsters like sea serpents, trolls, and gorgons. You can fight these creatures to collect rare items and craft powerful weapons and armor.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **IAFEnvoy**, **uoay**, **xiaowu**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
